# Jim Simons

**Rarity**: Legendary | **Archetype**: Pure Math | **Era**: 1938–2024

> "We don't override the models. The model is the system."

The Quant God. Renaissance Technologies' Medallion Fund returned ~66% annually before fees from 1988–2018 — the greatest track record in investing history. Simons proved that markets have exploitable statistical structure invisible to human intuition.

## Trading Philosophy

Simons doesn't have a trading philosophy in the traditional sense. He has a **scientific method**:

1. **Data over narrative** — No macro views, no fundamental analysis, no opinions about companies. Only patterns in data.
2. **The model is sovereign** — Human override is the enemy. If the model says trade, trade. If it says don't, don't. No exceptions.
3. **Signal decay is real** — Every edge degrades as markets adapt. You need a pipeline of new signals, not a single strategy.
4. **Hire scientists, not traders** — Renaissance hired mathematicians, physicists, and computer scientists. Zero Wall Street experience required.

## Key Facts

- **Medallion Fund**: ~66% annual returns before fees (39% after), 1988–2018. No losing year. Sharpe ratio estimated >6.
- **Closed to outsiders**: Medallion only manages Renaissance employees' money. Outside investors get RIEF and RIDA, which perform like ordinary quant funds.
- **Chern-Simons theory**: Before finance, Simons was a world-class mathematician. The Chern-Simons form is foundational in theoretical physics and topology.
- **Code-breaking**: Worked at IDA (Institute for Defense Analyses) breaking codes during the Cold War.

## The Medallion Mystery

Nobody outside Renaissance knows exactly how Medallion works. What's known:
- High-frequency and medium-frequency statistical arbitrage
- Thousands of small, uncorrelated bets
- Massive data infrastructure (market data, weather, news, satellite)
- Extremely low capacity (~$10B AUM, profits distributed annually)
- [[kelly-criterion]] likely underlies their position sizing

## Relevance to Vibe Sensei

As a guardian, Simons is the voice of systematic discipline. He warns against discretionary overrides, narrative-driven trading, and ignoring statistical evidence. His alerts ask: "What does the data say? Have you backtested? What's the sample size?"
