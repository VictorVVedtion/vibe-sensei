# Nassim Taleb

**Rarity**: Epic | **Archetype**: Antifragile | **Era**: 1960–present

> "Wind extinguishes a candle and energizes fire. Be the fire."

The Black Swan philosopher-trader. Taleb built both a practical trading framework (barbell strategy, tail-risk hedging) and a philosophical system ([[antifragility]]) that reframes how we think about risk, uncertainty, and survival.

## Trading Philosophy

Taleb's framework is about **surviving what you can't predict**:

1. **[[Antifragility]]** — Don't just be robust (survive shocks). Be antifragile (benefit from shocks). Structure positions so volatility helps you.
2. **Barbell strategy** — 85-90% in ultra-safe assets (T-bills, cash), 10-15% in extremely speculative bets (deep OTM options). Nothing in the middle. The middle is where you get killed by tail risk.
3. **Black Swans** — Rare, unpredictable, high-impact events dominate outcomes. Most risk models ignore them. Taleb builds his entire strategy around them.
4. **Skin in the game** — Don't take advice from people who don't bear the consequences. Applied to trading: never trust a model that its creator doesn't trade with their own money.

## Key Concepts

- **Black Swan Theory**: Events that are rare, unpredictable, and have massive impact. 9/11, 2008 crisis, COVID. Traditional finance treats these as outliers; Taleb treats them as the main event.
- **Fat tails**: Real market returns follow power-law distributions, not Gaussian. Standard deviation is a lie. This invalidates most of modern portfolio theory.
- **Lindy Effect**: Things that have survived a long time will likely survive longer. Old strategies > new strategies, all else equal.
- **Via Negativa**: Improvement by subtraction. Remove fragilities rather than adding features. In trading: reduce exposure to ruin rather than seeking alpha.

## Key Trades

- **1987 Crash**: Made a fortune on out-of-the-money puts during Black Monday. The trade that validated his entire worldview.
- **Universa Investments**: Co-founded with Mark Spitznagel. The fund is a permanent tail-risk hedge — it bleeds small amounts in normal markets but explodes during crashes. Made 3,612% in March 2020 (COVID crash).

## Relevance to Vibe Sensei

As a guardian, Taleb is the voice of survival. He doesn't care if your trade is right — he cares if you can survive being wrong. His alerts focus on: tail risk exposure, position sizing relative to total ruin, and whether you're in the fragile middle of the barbell.
