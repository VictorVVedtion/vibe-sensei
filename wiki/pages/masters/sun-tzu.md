# 孙子 Sun Tzu

**Rarity**: Legendary | **Archetype**: Strategic Positioning | **Era**: ~544–496 BC

> "知己知彼，百战不殆。" (Know yourself and know your enemy, and you will never be defeated.)

The supreme strategist. *The Art of War* is the most translated strategy text in history and its principles map directly to trading: positioning before battle, patience, deception, and knowing when not to fight.

## Trading Philosophy (Applied)

Sun Tzu never traded, but his principles are the DNA of tactical trading:

1. **Win before fighting** — "Every battle is won before it's ever fought." In trading: the setup determines the outcome. If you need luck, you've already lost.
2. **Positioning > force** — Don't fight the market. Position yourself where the market must come to you. This is the limit order mentality.
3. **Know the terrain** — Understand the market structure, the order book, the macro environment. [[george-soros]]'s reflexivity is a form of terrain analysis.
4. **Patience is a weapon** — "He who is prudent and lies in wait for an enemy who is not, will be victorious." [[warren-buffett]]'s cash position is Sun Tzu in practice.
5. **Retreat is not defeat** — Cutting losses is strategic withdrawal, not failure. [[jesse-livermore]] knew this intellectually but failed to practice it.

## Key Principles Applied to Markets

| Sun Tzu Principle | Market Application |
|---|---|
| "Appear weak when you are strong" | Don't signal your position to the market |
| "All warfare is based on deception" | Market makers, dark pools, spoofing |
| "Attack where the enemy is unprepared" | Contrarian entries during panic/euphoria |
| "The supreme art is to subdue the enemy without fighting" | Winning trades that never needed to be defended |

## Why a Non-Trader Is Legendary

Sun Tzu is the meta-strategist. He doesn't tell you *what* to trade — he tells you *how to think* about trading. His framework applies to:
- Risk management (never fight a battle you can't win → [[position-sizing]])
- Psychology (know yourself → emotional discipline)
- Timing (wait for the right moment → patience)
- Information advantage (know the enemy → research)

## Relevance to Vibe Sensei

As a guardian, Sun Tzu is the voice of strategic patience. He asks: Have you positioned yourself correctly? Do you understand both sides of this trade? Are you fighting a battle you've already won, or hoping to win a battle you haven't prepared for?
