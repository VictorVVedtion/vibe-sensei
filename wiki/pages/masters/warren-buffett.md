# Warren Buffett

**Rarity**: Legendary | **Archetype**: Value/Patience | **Era**: 1930–present

> "Rule #1: Never lose money. Rule #2: Never forget Rule #1."

The Oracle of Omaha. The most successful long-term investor in history, with a track record spanning 60+ years. Student of [[benjamin-graham]], but evolved the philosophy from pure cigar-butt value into quality compounders.

## Trading Philosophy

Buffett is anti-trading. His edge is **not trading**:

1. **[[Margin of safety]]** — Only buy when the price is significantly below intrinsic value. Inherited from [[benjamin-graham]], refined over a lifetime.
2. **Compounding is magic** — "My wealth has come from a combination of living in America, some lucky genes, and compound interest." Time in market >> timing the market.
3. **Circle of competence** — Only invest in what you understand. Say no to everything outside the circle, no matter how tempting.
4. **Be greedy when others are fearful** — Contrarian by temperament, not by strategy. The math works when everyone else panics.
5. **Hold forever** — "Our favorite holding period is forever." Reduces transaction costs, taxes, and emotional decision-making to near zero.

## Key Trades

- **American Express (1964)**: Bought during the Salad Oil Scandal when the stock crashed. Recognized the brand franchise was intact. Classic fear-based buying.
- **Coca-Cola (1988)**: Built a $1B position after the 1987 crash. Has never sold. Worth $25B+ today.
- **Apple (2016–)**: Berkshire's largest position. Buffett recognized Apple as a consumer franchise, not a tech stock. Against type but perfectly on-thesis.
- **Bank of America (2011)**: Invested $5B during the European debt crisis, structured as preferred stock + warrants. Classic Buffett: providing liquidity at a premium when others can't.

## The Graham Evolution

[[Benjamin-graham]] taught Buffett to buy cheap. [[Charlie-munger]] taught him to buy quality. The synthesis: **buy wonderful companies at fair prices**, rather than fair companies at wonderful prices. This evolution is one of the most important intellectual transitions in investing history.

## Relevance to Vibe Sensei

As a guardian, Buffett is the voice of patience and capital preservation. He warns against overtrading, leverage, and chasing momentum. His alerts focus on: "Would you be comfortable holding this if the exchange closed for 5 years?" If no, don't buy it.
