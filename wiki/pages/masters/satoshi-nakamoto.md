# Satoshi Nakamoto

**Rarity**: Legendary | **Archetype**: Trustless | **Era**: 2008–2011 (active)

> "If you don't believe me or don't get it, I don't have time to convince you."

The pseudonymous creator of Bitcoin. Published the whitepaper on October 31, 2008, launched the network on January 3, 2009, then vanished in 2011 — never moving their estimated ~1M BTC. The disappearance is as much a part of the philosophy as the code.

## Trading Philosophy (Inferred)

Satoshi never traded. The philosophy is embedded in the system design:

1. **Trustless systems** — "The root problem with conventional currency is all the trust that's required to make it work." Remove trust requirements entirely via cryptographic proof.
2. **Fixed supply** — 21 million BTC, ever. The hardest money ever created. This is a bet against central bank monetary policy and a direct challenge to [[powell]] and [[yellen]].
3. **Verify, don't trust** — Every node validates every transaction. No intermediaries. This is the opposite of [[sbf]]'s FTX, where trust was the entire model.
4. **The exit** — Satoshi's disappearance removed the single point of failure. A leader who leaves is more powerful than a leader who stays. This echoes [[laozi]]: "A leader is best when people barely know they exist."

## Key Moments

- **2008-10-31**: Bitcoin whitepaper published to the cryptography mailing list.
- **2009-01-03**: Genesis block mined. Embedded message: "The Times 03/Jan/2009 Chancellor on brink of second bailout for banks."
- **2010-12-12**: Last known forum post. Quietly stopped contributing.
- **~1M BTC never moved**: The coins sitting untouched since 2009 are the ultimate HODL — and the ultimate proof of commitment to the protocol over personal gain.

## Impact on Markets

Bitcoin created an entirely new asset class and spawned the crypto ecosystem that includes every crypto-era master in Vibe Sensei: [[arthur-hayes]], [[vitalik-buterin]], [[cz-zhao]], [[michael-saylor]], and the ghosts ([[ftx-collapse]], [[three-arrows-capital]]).

The genesis block message is the thesis statement: traditional finance is broken, and the alternative is trustless, permissionless, and mathematically constrained.
