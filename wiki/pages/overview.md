## Core Themes

The wiki organizes trading knowledge around recurring themes:

1. **Risk Management** — Position sizing, drawdown control, Kelly criterion, margin of safety
2. **Market Psychology** — Fear/greed cycles, FOMO, reflexivity, madness of crowds
3. **Systematic vs. Discretionary** — Quant models vs. intuition, when to override
4. **Contrarian Thinking** — Buying when others sell, the value of being wrong
5. **Leverage** — Double-edged sword across every era (Livermore, 3AC, Archegos, LTCM)
6. **Eastern Philosophy Applied** — Wu wei, patience, counter-cyclical timing
7. **Cautionary Tales** — What happens when conviction becomes hubris

## How to Use This Wiki

**Ingest sources**: Drop articles, papers, book chapters, or transcripts into `raw/` and ask the LLM to process them. Each ingest updates relevant master, concept, and market pages.

**Ask questions**: Query the wiki for synthesized answers. "How would Soros and Buffett disagree on this trade?" — the wiki has the context to answer.

**Lint periodically**: Ask for a health check to find gaps, contradictions, and missing connections.
